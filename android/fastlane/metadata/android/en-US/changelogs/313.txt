Unlock is one tap: the fingerprint prompt comes up by itself when the machine asks, and names it. Cancelling it brings back the Approve screen.

- The status dot checks every minute; a long press on it checks now.
- Pairing and unlocking say what they are doing and how long is left to approve.
- A failed unlock says what the discovery server last knew of the machine.
- Keyboard shortcuts and screen reader labels throughout.
- If the app says the key must be replaced, pair again.
