First build of the synctang key holder.

- Pair with a machine by scanning its QR code, or by typing its device
  ID, and get the exact enrol command to run on the machine.
- One Unlock action: the machine's verified identity is shown, a strong
  biometric is required, and the phone answers the challenge with a
  single elliptic curve agreement performed inside the Keystore.
- The long-term key is generated in StrongBox where the phone has a
  secure element, and in the trusted execution environment otherwise.
  It cannot be exported either way.
- The machine's disk passphrase is never on the phone, in any form.
- Confirm-code mode is supported: when the machine prints a code on its
  own console, it has to be checked on the phone before anything is
  answered.
- No tracking, no analytics, no proprietary libraries.
